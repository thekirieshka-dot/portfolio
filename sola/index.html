(01)The idea

Sun is not the enemy. It is the weather we live in. We make four formulas for the hours after it: barrier first, gentle actives second, and nothing that stings.

We started SOLA in 2021 after three summers working outdoors on the Alentejo coast.

Every formula is short enough to read on the bottle. Every bottle is amber glass you can send back. Nothing is scented to hide what is inside.

Step 01 — Cleanse

Begin with oil.

Two pumps of Night Oil on dry skin. Massage for a full minute while sunscreen, salt and the city lift away. Warm water turns it milky; a linen cloth takes it off.

Night Oil · 30 ml · $52

Step 02 — Treat

Three drops, pressed.

Amber Drop goes onto skin that is still a little damp. Squalane and niacinamide settle in within a minute; bakuchiol gets to work on texture. No tingle, no tightness.

Amber Drop · 30 ml · $58

Step 03 — Restore

Seal it in, slowly.

A pea of Clay Barrier Cream, warmed between your palms. Shea and ceramides hold everything in place overnight, so skin wakes up soft rather than shiny.

Clay Barrier Cream · 50 ml · $64
