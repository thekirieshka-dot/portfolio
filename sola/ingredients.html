A

  • 01

    Allantoin

    INCI · Allantoin

    A calming compound first found in comfrey root, now made without it. It takes the sting out of skin that has had too much wind.

    Soothing0.3%
    • Clay Barrier Cream
  • 02

    Aqua

    INCI · Aqua

    Water, filtered and deionised. It is the first line of the serum and the cream, so it gets the same scrutiny as everything after it.

    Base—
    • Amber Drop
    • Clay Barrier Cream

B

  • 03

    Bakuchiol

    INCI · Bakuchiol

    A plant-derived alternative to retinol that works on the look of texture without the peeling, so it can be worn in daylight.

    From Madhya Pradesh, India

    Active1%
    • Amber Drop

C

  • 04

    Calendula extract

    INCI · Calendula Officinalis Flower Extract

    Marigold petals steeped in sunflower oil. A traditional comfort for skin that has been out too long.

    Soothing<1%
    • Night Oil

    Candelilla wax

    INCI · Euphorbia Cerifera (Candelilla) Wax

    A plant wax from a desert shrub. It holds the balm together on a hot day and makes it vegan, because no beeswax is needed.

    From Coahuila, Mexico

    Texture12%
    • Cheek & Lip Balm

    • Cheek & Lip Balm

    Barrier—

  • 09

    Cetearyl alcohol

    INCI · Cetearyl Alcohol

    A fatty alcohol, not a drying one. It gives the cream its body and keeps the oil and water in it from parting.

    INCI · Citric Acid

    Keeps fresh<0.1%
    • Amber Drop

    • Amber Drop

(Left out)Ten things you will not find

What we leave out.

  1. 01

    Added fragrance

    Not in anything, ever. Scent is the commonest reason skin complains, and the sun makes it worse.

  2. 02

    Essential oils

    Lovely on a shelf, lively on skin. The citrus ones react in sunlight, which is rather the point of a sun brand.

  3. 03

    Mineral oil

    A petroleum by-product that sits on skin rather than joining it. Jojoba and squalane do the same job better.

  4. 04

    Sulfates

    We make no foaming cleanser, so we have no use for SLS or SLES.

  5. 05

    Parabens

    Small batches, dated by hand, twelve months once opened. We do not need them.

  6. 06

    Silicones

    Slip without substance. Squalane gives the same glide and washes away.

  7. 07

    Denatured alcohol

    Feels fresh for a minute, dries the barrier for an hour. Not here.

  8. 08

    Microplastics

    No beads, no glitter, no synthetic polymers in the formula and none in the box.

  9. 09

    Beeswax and lanolin

    All four formulas are vegan. Candelilla holds the balm together instead.

  10. 10

    Synthetic dyes

    The balm’s apricot is iron oxide: earth, not dye. The rest is the colour of the oil.
