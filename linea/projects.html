Sheet 01 / 05Public · 2023Built

Arcade at Lindevang, Frederiksberg

A covered walk along the edge of the park. Fourteen bays of vaults in lime-rendered brick give shade in July and shelter in November.

The vaults were built by hand on timber centring, one bay a week. The render is three coats of hydraulic lime, left white and never painted, so it weathers to the colour of the park path. From inside, the sky is cut into pieces small enough to look at.

Sheet 02 / 05House · 2022Built

Pine House, Gilleleje

Three stacked volumes set back from the one pine on the plot. The house was drawn around the tree.

Every room has a view of the trunk, and none of them looks at the neighbours. The upper floor cantilevers four metres to keep the roots free of foundations, and the terrace steps down with the grade rather than levelling it.

214 m²
Year

Location

Structure

Sheet 03 / 05Interior · 2021Built

Curtain Apartment, Frederiksberg

A fourth-floor flat with thick walls and too many doors. We closed four of them and hung linen where they used to be.

Two arched niches were carved into the party wall, deep enough to sit in. The plaster is polished lime, burnished with stones over six weeks. The rooms now open and close with a hand, not a hinge.

Area
118 m²
Year
2021
Location
Frederiksberg, Copenhagen
Structure
Existing 1904 brick building
Materials
Tadelakt lime, oak floor, Belgian linen
Status
Built
“The client asked for more space. We gave her fewer doors.”Mats Lindqvist, partner
Sheet 04 / 05Public · 2024Built

Kastrup Municipal Hall, Tårnby

A civic floor lifted on a field of thin black columns, leaving the ground open as a covered square.

The hall above takes daylight from four sides and is organised around a single counter, 42 metres long, so that every visit begins at the same table. Below, the square holds a weekly market and the bicycles of everyone who works upstairs.

Area
2 850 m²
Year
2024
Location
Kastrup, Tårnby
Structure
Post-tensioned slab on 36 blackened steel columns
Materials
White concrete, steel, terrazzo
Status

Sheet 05 / 05House · 2025Built

Stair House, Aarhus

A narrow house organised around one stair. The treads are cast in lime and the walls are rendered by hand.

Light from a single roof window grades slowly from white to sand as it falls three storeys. Nothing on the stair is fixed to the walls: the handrail is the wall, rounded where the hand goes.

Area
168 m²
Year
2025
Location
Risskov, Aarhus
Structure
Load-bearing brick, cast lime stair
Materials
Lime plaster inside and out, oak treads above
Status
Built
“The stair is the only corridor. It had to be a room as well.”Ida Thorsen, partner

06 — Helsingør Lime Works

A kiln hall becomes a reading room.

A 1920s lime works on the harbour, closed since 1971. We keep the brick shell and the chimney, open the arched windows that were bricked up in the fifties, and put a long table under each of them.
