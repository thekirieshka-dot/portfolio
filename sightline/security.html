(03)What we never store

Eight things, crossed out.

  • N-01

    Row-level data after 24 hours

    The cache expires in the storage layer, not on a schedule someone has to remember.

  • N-02

    Warehouse admin credentials

    We hold one read-only role. Anything wider is refused when you connect.

  • N-03

    Unmasked personal columns

    Columns tagged pii in your semantic layer are hashed before the planner sees them.

  • N-04

    Your prompts in a model provider’s logs

    Zero-retention inference. It is in the contract, not a setting someone can flip.

  • N-05

    Card numbers

    Payments sit with the processor. We keep the last four digits and the invoice.

  • N-06

    Screenshots of your dashboards

    Charts are drawn from SQL every time they open. There is no image to leak.

  • N-07

    Tables you did not grant

    They never appear in the semantic layer, so nobody can ask about them by accident.

  • N-08

    Your data in a second region

    A workspace lives in Frankfurt or Oregon. Nothing replicates across.
