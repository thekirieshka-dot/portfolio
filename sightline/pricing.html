(04)Billing

Small print, large type.

The questions finance asks, answered before they ask them.

One message you send that Sightline answers. Follow-ups count. Opening a saved answer, refreshing a dashboard or a scheduled Slack post does not: those re-run a query you already paid for.

Nothing breaks. Questions keep getting answered, and the extra ones are billed at $0.12 each, dropping to $0.07 after 25,000. The admin gets a note at 80% and again at 100%, so there are no surprises on the invoice.

Only people who type questions. Anyone who opens a shared link, reads a Slack digest or comments on an answer is a viewer, and viewers are unlimited and free. Most teams find about one in four employees ends up asking.

You export the semantic layer as plain YAML from git, and the saved answers with their SQL as a CSV. Then we delete the workspace and send a certificate within 30 days. Your warehouse was never touched, so there is nothing to migrate back.
