(03)Questions

Before you send it.

Every six to eight years, which is how long a good oil lasts on a pivot. Sooner if the rate wanders by more than ten seconds a day, if it stops for no reason, or if it has been somewhere wet with the crown unscrewed.

About ten weeks, door to door. Of those, one is on the timing bench at the end and the rest is mostly the queue: there are six of us, and each service is done by one person, start to finish.

Every Calibre M1, whoever owns it. The initials under the barrel bridge tell us which bench built it, and that watchmaker, or the next in line, takes it back.

We quote after we have opened it, and before we do any work. The quote comes with photographs of what we found. If the answer is “nothing needs doing”, you pay for the postage.

Only what is worn past tolerance, and always the mainspring. Whatever we take out is returned to you in a small paper envelope, with a note of where it came from.
