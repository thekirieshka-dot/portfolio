Typical, not promised.

(04) Why drawings?

We don’t publish patient photographs.

A smile belongs to the person wearing it. So every case on this page is drawn from a real treatment record — the shade tab at the start and end, the positions of the teeth, the measurements — with nothing else carried across.

  1. i.Shades are matched in daylight at the chair, never under a ring light.
  2. ii.Photographs are taken for your own plan and kept in your notes, not on this website.
  3. iii.If you’d like to see an after-photograph of your kind of case, ask your dentist — some patients choose to share theirs.
