Your first visit Forty-five minutes No waiting room

Your first visit.

Five calm steps, in the order they happen. Nothing is done that you haven’t agreed to, and you can stop at any point.

Scroll — it takes about a minute to read

  2. 01

    The door

    Ring the bell, that’s it.

    There’s no waiting room. Ring the bell on the mews side and your dentist comes to the door, then walks you straight through. The entrance is step-free, and if you’re early, so are we.

    How long
    2 minutes
    With
    Your dentist
    Bring
    A list of any medicines you take

  3. 02

    Sitting up

    You talk first.

    You start in an ordinary chair, not the reclined one. We ask what bothers you, what you’d like and what you’d hate. Nobody opens a mouth mirror until we’ve heard all of it.

    How long
    10 minutes
    With
    Your dentist
    Bring
    Any old X-rays or letters, if you have them

  4. 03

    The look

    A proper, slow look.

    Teeth, gums, bite and jaw, one at a time, with photographs if you’d like them. Digital X-rays only when they’re needed. Everything we see goes up on the screen beside you, so you see it too.

    How long
    20 minutes
    With
    Your dentist
    Bring
    Questions — write them in your phone

  5. 04

    The options

    Plain English, no pressure.

    We explain what we found and what, if anything, we’d do. Often the first option is “nothing yet”. We agree a hand signal for “stop”, and it works at every visit after this one.

    How long
    10 minutes
    With
    Your dentist
    Bring
    Nothing. Just you

  6. 05

    The plan

    A plan to take away.

    Within 48 hours you have it in writing: the options, the number of visits, the full cost and what we would choose for our own teeth. You don’t book anything until you’ve slept on it.

    How long
    3 minutes, then 48 hours
    With
    Noor, at the desk
    Bring
    Nothing to pay online

(06) If you’re nervous

Tell us at the door. We’ll slow everything down, say what is about to happen before it does, and stop the moment you lift a hand. Many of our longest-standing patients started out exactly where you are.

(07) A few last things

Before you come.

Forty-five minutes for a first consultation. If you’d like to talk through something complicated, say so when you book and we’ll give you a longer slot.

Only if we can’t answer a question without them. Ours are low-dose and digital, and if you’ve had recent ones elsewhere, bring the images and we’ll use those instead.

Nothing is taken online when you book. Your written plan sets out every cost before any treatment, and nothing changes after you’ve agreed it.

Of course. A friend, a partner or a parent is welcome in the room for the conversation, and can sit in the surgery for the rest if you’d like.

Ring the bell anyway: we’ll do what we can in the time left. To move a visit, reply to your reminder text at least 48 hours before and there’s no charge.

Ready when you are

Pick a time that suits you.

Two minutes online, nothing to pay, free to move up to 48 hours before. We’ll text to confirm.
