(03) How we train

Better, slowly.

Nothing here is clever. It is a handful of habits, kept up for twelve years.

01

Forty hours a year

The professional minimum is a hundred hours of study every five years. We do about forty a year, and we do it on weekdays, not in the fortnight before a deadline.

Every clinician
02

Mock-up Mondays

On the first Monday of each month the two dentists plan a case together at one screen: shapes, lengths, shades. A second opinion is written into the diary, not left to chance.

Monthly
03

Six weeks at an elbow

Anyone new spends six weeks beside a colleague before treating alone. Noor did the same before she was let near the diary.

Every new starter
04

Loupes, always

3.5× magnification for every restorative visit, with a headlight. You see far more at three and a half times, and so do we.

Every restorative visit
05

A quarterly look back

One Friday afternoon each quarter, the diary is closed and we review the before-and-afters on the wall: what we would repeat, what we would redo.

Quarterly
