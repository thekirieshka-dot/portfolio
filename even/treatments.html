Treatments Cosmetic & preventive 06 treatments

Six treatments, one dentist, no rush.

We don’t offer everything. We offer six things we’re very good at — and we’ll tell you honestly when you don’t need any of them.

Hover a treatment to preview itTap a treatment for the details

Custom-fitted trays and a low-strength gel you wear at home for about two weeks, after we’ve cleaned and checked every tooth. Slower than a one-hour blast, far kinder to sensitive teeth, and the result lasts.

Visits
Two — a fitting, then a review
Time
45 minutes, then two weeks at home
Aftercare
Keep your trays. A top-up every year or so; go easy on red wine for 48 hours.
Suits
Teeth yellowed by age, tea or coffee. Crowns and fillings won’t change colour.
Book a whitening fit

Wafer-thin porcelain, shaped by hand and bonded to the front of the tooth to change its shape, length or colour. You try the new shape on your own teeth with a mock-up first, and preparation is kept to a fraction of a millimetre.

Visits
Three or four
Time
Six to eight weeks, start to finish
Aftercare
Brush and floss as normal; a night guard if you grind. Expect fifteen years or more.
Suits
Chipped, worn or uneven front teeth that whitening alone can’t fix.
Book a veneer consultation

A series of near-invisible trays that move your teeth a fraction of a millimetre at a time. We scan, plan the movement with you on screen, and check in every six to eight weeks — always with the same dentist.

Visits
A scan, then every six to eight weeks
Time
Four to twelve months, typically
Aftercare
Retainers at night, for good. Your first pair is included.
Suits
Mild crowding, small gaps, or teeth that moved after braces as a teenager.
Book an aligner scan

Tooth-coloured resin sculpted straight onto the tooth, layer by layer, to close a gap, rebuild a chipped edge or even out a shape. No drilling and, most of the time, no injection.

Visits
One or two
Time
60–90 minutes per visit
Aftercare
A polish at each hygiene visit; we re-gloss it every year or so.
Suits
Single chips, small gaps and worn edges — or a reversible trial before veneers.
Book bonding

A full hour with our hygienist: gentle scaling, air polishing to lift stains, and honest advice for your mouth rather than a generic leaflet. The appointment everyone underrates.

Visits
Every four to six months
Time
60 minutes
Aftercare
Nothing beyond keeping it up. Teeth can feel sensitive to cold for a day.
Suits
Everyone — and it keeps whitening and bonding looking new.

How a visit works

Five steps, no surprises.

Whatever brings you in, the shape of it is the same. Nothing is done on the day you hear about it, and nothing costs more than the number on your plan.

  2. 01

    Book online

    Choose a treatment — or a consultation if you’re not sure — and a time that suits. We text a reminder the day before.

    Two minutes
  3. 02

    A proper conversation

    We listen first: what bothers you, what you’d like, what you’d hate. Then a full check, photographs, and a 3D scan if it helps.

    45 minutes
  4. 03

    A plan on paper

    Options, number of visits, the full cost and what we’d choose for our own teeth — written in plain English, yours to take away and think about.

    Within 48 hours
  5. 04

    Treatment, same dentist

    The dentist who planned it does it. Appointments are long enough that nobody is glancing at the clock, and you can stop at any point.

    At your pace
  6. 05

    A look back

    We review the result together, take after photographs, and adjust anything that isn’t quite right — at no charge.

    At three months
