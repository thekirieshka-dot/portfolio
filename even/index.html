(02) How we work

One dentist, start to finish.

Most clinics pass you along: one person to sell, another to treat, a third to check. We don’t. Whoever plans your treatment carries it out and sees it through.

  1. i.

    Natural, not new.

    We match shape, translucency and shade to the teeth you already have. The aim is for people to say you look well — not that you’ve had something done.

  2. ii.

    Unhurried, on purpose.

    Forty minutes for a check-up, a full hour for hygiene. If you have questions, there’s time to answer them properly, in plain English.

  3. iii.

    The same face, every visit.

    No hand-offs and no repeating your story. Your dentist keeps your photographs, your plan and your preferences — down to which radio station you don’t want on.

(03) Shade guide

What colour are your teeth, really?

Dentists judge tooth colour against porcelain shade tabs, lightest to darkest. Pick the one closest to yours, then see where professional whitening could sensibly take it.

A3 Warm natural

The shade we see most in tea and coffee drinkers over forty. Healthy, warm, and very responsive to whitening.

Screen colours are approximate. We match shades in daylight at the chair, never under a ring light.
